Valley Band sketch

A synthesized sketch of the Valley Band from the audio spec: harmony, grooves and layering, played live from a small Songbook. Everything you hear is built with Web Audio, no samples. The game itself will use sampled acoustic instruments, so treat the timbres as a stand-in and listen to the chords, the feel and how the band grows.
