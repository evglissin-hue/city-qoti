## Try during the evening

1. Start a New valley for two with the founding guide on, and follow Tobias Merriweather's ten letters. Did each letter say what to do, and could you do it? Note the day each letter ticked.
2. Ledger: zone Homes, Workshops and Farmland from the Build picker's Zones shelf (zone Farmland deep, so grain farms have room for fields), place a Forester's Lodge by the woods, a Fishery on the river, a Quarry on grey stone. Hover the four demand bars: do the words explain what the town wants?
3. Watch a business grow on a lot: which trade did it take, who keeps it, and does its look say so (sign, goods out front)? Open the Businesses book (L) and the inspector.
4. Open Balances (M): the five families, days of cover, flavors on offer, the carrier's rule, reserve and selling switch. Change a rule, then undo it (Ctrl+Z).
5. Hearth: grant wishes, discover a pattern, plant a garden bed by a door, set a bench by the river (it faces the water), drag lanterns along a lane, pick something up with Move and set it down.
6. Claim the Village Charter (30 residents, 3 patterns, 5 wishes, 5 days of Food with 3 flavors, a season in surplus). How many evenings did it take?
7. Answer the season card's questions in summer and autumn (the winter reserve, a deal, where the builders go first), then live through the first winter. Were households fed and warm? Did the closed pass bite?
8. Try a mistake on purpose: remove a business, or set Food to Never, and undo it.
9. Close both tabs and reopen the next day: is the valley exactly as you left it?

## Questions

- What was the best moment? What felt like a chore?
- Was anything confusing? Where did you get stuck?
- Did each lane have enough to do? Did you need each other?
- Did the town feel like it built, staffed and supplied itself, or did you have to push it?
- When did the first Craftsfolk move up, and what held the others back (the Next tier line in a home's inspector)?
- Did the treasury ever go into the red? What pulled it back?
- Bugs, desyncs, performance on each laptop (fps from the debug overlay).
- Three things to change before the next iteration.

## What R1X saw from the player's chair (2026-11-12)
