## What to look for in the evening (V4)

- **Stop and look.** Rest on a street for a second: the shadows soften and the edges settle, with no flash or jump as it develops. Let a lamp light or a building rise while you rest: the picture should take it in without popping back to a rougher frame.
- **Photo mode** (the camera button by the minimap): drag the hour and the depth of field, Save PNG, and open the file. Its sliders should look like the rest of the interface, by day and at lamplight.
- **The books**: open the Pattern Book and the Chronicle; their pictures are your own valley in ink and wash. No two pictures in a row should be the same.
- **The endpaper map**: zoom all the way out and the valley becomes its drawn map. At night it takes a faint lamplit wash, and the evening's footfall over the roofs reads lighter than before.
- **Tea break**: leave the game alone for two minutes, once by day and once in the evening. The camera wanders, the interface steps back to the town's name and the clock, and any touch brings both back exactly where you left them. Settings has a switch for it.
