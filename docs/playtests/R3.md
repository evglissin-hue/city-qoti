## Try during the evening

1. **The first lamplit Village evening.** Claim the Village Charter, build a chandler, and stand a dozen lanterns along two places. Watch 17:30 to 18:00: the lamplighter leaves the Town Hall with his pole, and each lamp comes on as he reaches it. With candles short, open each place's card: which places are kept first, which rest tonight, and does the card say why?
2. **A priority set and undone.** On a place card, keep one place first; on another, let it rest. Undo both (Ctrl+Z). Then switch the carrier to buy for places in Balances, and undo that. Does each change show on both laptops?
3. **A winter evening with and without firewood.** Put a brazier by a busy bench. One evening with firewood in store, one without (sell it or let the carrier keep it). Who stays in, and does the place card say the fire is cold?
4. **Masters.** Keep a baker or a cider maker busy at level 2 for a few weeks. When a master comes, find the sign at the door, the master's good on a shelf and the line in the Businesses book. Who apprentices under them? Then let the master leave town (or move them to other work): does their good stop, and does the business keep its level for a few days before it slides?
5. **Buildings that grow.** Watch a forester's lodge, a quarry or a fishery climb a level. From the default camera, can you tell its level without opening it (the pennants on its pole)? Does the inspector say what the next level needs?
6. **The Town Book together.** Open the Town Book (Books shelf, U). Read the three hints, and between you work toward one: the Hearth's player lays the place, the Ledger's player sets up the trade. When a page is found, whose names does it carry? Does a calling come with three pages of one leaning, and does the Charter board say so?
7. **Follow the thread.** Open a cider maker, a home and a place: does the Thread read where the cider came from and where it went, and does its route draw on the ground? Click a name in the sentence.
8. **The storybook.** Close a chapter and press "Print the storybook" on the Chapter card. Print to PDF at A4 or Letter: cover map, the Chronicle as a gazette, the Pattern Book and Town Book pages, portraits by household. Anything cut off, blank or misspelled? Note page and line.
9. **Hands and food.** Build workplaces first and homes late, as a newcomer would. Do farms and fisheries keep their hands while food is short, and does anyone miss breakfast in the Hamlet?
10. **The money.** After the Village Charter, watch the treasury and the loans for a season. When the first master's goods leave on the carrier's cart, does the treasury turn? Do households start putting coins by (a home's Next tier line)?
11. Close both tabs and reopen the next day: is the valley exactly as you left it, lamps, masters and pages included?

## Questions

- What was the best moment? What felt like a chore?
- Did a master feel like someone the town had earned? Was the sign worth the wait?
- Did the Town Book's hints read as clues you could act on, or as riddles? Which hint did you follow, and did it land?
- Did lamplight and resting pieces change where folk went, and could you tell why from the place card?
- Did any line in the place card, the Thread, the Town Book or the storybook jar? Note it word for word.
- When did the first Craftsfolk household move up, and what held the others back (the Next tier line in a home's inspector)?
- Did the treasury live on the County Bank for long after the Village Charter? What pulled it back?
- Bugs, desyncs, performance on each laptop.
