## Try during the evening

1. Play on Medium first, then try High and Ultra ("Spare no candles") in Settings.
2. Look closely: zoom right in on a street, a thatched cottage and a brick shop. Do the bricks, tiles and thatch read? Do the windows have frames and sills?
3. Zoom out slowly over a village: does anything pop as it swaps to its far version?
4. Stay up past dark: are the windows lit across the whole town, and do the lamp pools glow?
5. Watch golden hour (around 17:00 to 18:30 in the game).
6. Leave the camera still for a minute with residents walking: does it stay smooth and quiet (the fans should calm down)?
7. With `?debug`, glance at the overlay's `res`, the mode (moving, still, idle) and `stills`.
