## Try during the evening

1. One of you creates a new valley for two (standard is the default now) and sends the invite. Watch the two founding families walk in through the valley gate at 08:00.
2. Ledger: set up timber first (woodcutter at the forest edge, then a sawpit), then food (a vegetable plot, a fisher's hut by the river), then cottages for the next families. Watch porters carry bundles and sites rise through their four stages. Open Reports: stocks, workforce, budget.
3. Hearth: read the wishes (J), answer a few with benches, trees, flowers and lanterns, and watch for the hop and the thank-you letter. Open the Pattern Book (B) and try to find a page from its hints. Customize a cottage.
4. Pause a building and watch its workers find other jobs. Sell surplus to the peddler.
5. Let it run into dusk and night: lit windows, lantern pools, residents heading home.
6. Undo something with Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z). Save a valley file from Settings; open it from the title screen.
7. Leave for a day, come back, read the while-you-were-away letter.
8. Aim for 30 residents in two evenings.
