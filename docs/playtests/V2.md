## Try during the evening

1. **Night in the grown village**: open `?debug&fixture=grown&hour=22` on the V2X link. Zoom in on the high street, then pull back slowly to the valley's edge. Does every lantern light the ground and the wall beside it? Do the lit windows show little rooms when you are close? Does the town read as a glow from far off? Is anything too bright, too orange or flat?
2. **Night in your own valley**: start a fresh valley together and play until dark. Same questions, on a hamlet with a handful of lamps. Look at the river by a lamp.
3. **Trees and meadows in each season**: in the grown village try `&season=summer&day=3`, `&season=autumn&day=5` and `&season=winter&day=3` (add `&weather=clear`). At the default camera and at street zoom: do the crowns, hedges and meadows read as painted? Do the trees thin in autumn and stand bare in winter? Zoom in and out slowly over a meadow and a wood: does anything pop, flicker or show an edge?
4. **Close up on a street by day**: barrels, benches, carts, residents' clothes. Do wood grain, painted wood, iron, terracotta and cloth read? Do walls darken softly at their feet in narrow streets? Does chimney smoke rise in soft ribbons?
5. **The bubbles toggle**: in the Hearth's View list, **Moods**. Do the bubbles help or clutter?
6. **The numbers readout**: with `?debug`, glance at the overlay's mode (moving, still, idle), `res` and `stills` on Medium and on High, moving and at rest, at noon and at night.
7. Leave the camera still for a minute at night with residents walking: does it stay smooth and quiet (the fans should calm down)?
