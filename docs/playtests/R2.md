## Try during the evening

1. Start a New valley for two. Before the first evening (17:00), set a bench, a flower bed and a lantern together near the first homes. At 17:00 the clock slows (the lingering evening, Settings): watch folk plan, set off and sit. Open a resident (their Day) and a place (click its tiles): what does each say?
2. Read the Hearth's strip ("N of M out") each evening, and the evening edition at 21:00. Can the Ledger's player say from the edition what the Hearth did for them today, and the other way round?
3. Turn on Town in either lens's View list: the sage wash (where folk walk of an evening), the ochre dashes (where goods go by day), the glint at outlets that serve a place. Does it tell you where a shop would do well?
4. Ledger: zone Shops along the busiest evening walk. Does a shop grow there, and does its till fill on the round? Mark a stretch as an evening street (Ledger, Evening streets): do carts go round, and does the brush say which doors take no deliveries?
5. Claim the Village Charter (its evening goal: half the grown-ups out on three fair evenings). How many evenings did it take, and did the Charter board say what was missing?
6. Gaps: from Village rank, a place with nothing to sell, a shop nobody passes, a street with nowhere to go. Close one, and look for "Closed by" in the feed and the next edition.
7. A winter evening: with a brazier by a busy bench, and without. Who stays in?
8. Grow the town past twenty households with nowhere worth going of an evening: does the Homes bar (and the Ledger feed) say newcomers want somewhere to go? Does a place fix it within a few evenings?
9. Rename a place and a street, then undo each (Ctrl+Z).
10. Close both tabs and reopen the next day: is the valley exactly as you left it?

## Questions

- What was the best moment? What felt like a chore?
- Did each lane need the other? When did you first notice it?
- Were the evenings worth watching, or did you speed through them? Was the lingering evening too slow or too quick?
- Did the edition and the place card read as warm and dry, or did any line jar? Note it word for word.
- When did the first Craftsfolk household move up, and what held the others back (the Next tier line in a home's inspector)?
- Did the treasury go into the red after the Village Charter? What pulled it back?
- Bugs, desyncs, performance on each laptop (fps from the debug overlay at 19:00).
