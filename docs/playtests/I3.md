## The evening plan

A year is four seasons of six days. At the fastest speed a season takes about a quarter of an hour, so a year fits in an evening with time to look around; slow down for the festivals and the raid.

1. **Spring and summer: get ready.**
   - Hearth: open the Field Guide and the habitats overlay; dig a pond, plant a meadow by the woods, put up a bird box. Aim for **ten species** by the end of the year.
   - Ledger: plots and an orchard while the sun is up (nothing grows in winter); a chopping shed or two and a woodcutter for the woodpile; a Trading Post if there is none. Watch the season card for the forecast (flood, drought, early frost, cold snap, the circus).
   - Each season ends in a festival: plan it from the Festival book (site, menu, music, twist). Try for three stars in the first year and see what a fifth would take.
2. **Autumn: the larder and the woodpile.**
   - The First Winter (the Village Charter's trial) is weathered when no household goes a whole day without firewood and the larder never runs dry; it seals the Charter. Ledger: check the Reports for days of food and firewood ahead; Hearth: festival menus eat from the same larder, so agree on what to spend.
   - Build a Bath House near the homes if you can.
3. **Winter: the trial and the Winter Hearth Feast.**
   - Plan the **Winter Hearth Feast** together: a warm site, a hearty menu, a twist.
   - Watch the warmth overlay for cold homes. Skate on the frozen river (at the camera, not in person).
4. **A raid at dusk.**
   - When a raid is telegraphed (the threat band on the season card, tracks and signs), put up a palisade or a stone wall by the stores, light lanterns, staff the Watch House and set the guard points (Watch book).
   - Meet it at dusk: ring the bell, watch who goes where and what is taken. Afterward read the result card, mend what was damaged, and decide what to do with any captured bandits (they can join the town).
5. **Look around**: photo mode (a free camera, depth of field, a time of day of your own), a Chronicle entry with its picture, a module on a home (porch, dormer, balcony), the River Dock and a commission.

## Playtest flags: check these on purpose

The I1 and V1 evenings flagged these. Look at each under **snow, crowds and raids**:

- **Floaty walking**: do residents, raiders and the Watch plant their feet, or glide? Watch a crowd at the festival and a chase in the snow.
- **Retina performance**: fps and GPU ms on the Retina laptop with snow falling, at the festival and in the raid; does `res` step down while moving and recover at rest?
- **Plain menus**: do the Festival, Field Guide, Watch and Trial books feel like part of the storybook, or like forms?
- **Half-lit lanterns**: at dusk and at night, do the festival lights, the Watch's lanterns and the street lamps all light, and light the ground around them?
