## Try during the evening

1. **A grown street by day**: open `?debug&fixture=grown` and zoom right in on the high street (the play camera's nearest). Without opening a panel, can you tell who lives and works in each building: the baker's loaves on the sill, the dairy's churns by the door, a woodpile as big as a household's firewood, a painted sign at a level 2 business? Is anything floating, poking through a wall, standing on a road or repeated so often it reads as wallpaper?
2. **The same street at night**: add `&hour=22`. Do the street lamps, yard lamps and sill lamps light something (a doorway, a yard, a shop's goods)? Is any lamp standing alone on grass, lighting nothing?
3. **Rows and gardens**: in the console, `__qoti.streetsShowcase()` and look at the block it builds (center 96, 90): joined rows stepping up and down, a corner shop with its door in the cut corner, gardens behind with sheds, washing and hens. (On `main` these show in your own valley once R2's lots land with R2X.)
4. **A fresh valley**: start one together and play an hour. Do the first homes and businesses dress themselves as they grow?
5. **The long view in each season**: in the grown village pull the camera out slowly from the street to as far as it goes, by day and at golden hour, then with `&season=autumn&day=5`, `&season=winter&day=3` (add `&weather=clear`). Does the valley run up into wooded hills that fade into the sky, with no straight edge, wall or box? Does the river leave through a gorge and run on? Does the map take over between 200 and 240 tiles with no pop, no blank frame and no bare parchment?
6. **The river**: zoom in where it bends. Do the banks curve, or do they step with the tiles?
7. **The numbers readout**: with `?debug`, glance at the overlay's mode, `res`, `stills` and the `endpaper` line on Medium and on High, moving and at rest, at noon and at night, and under the full map.
