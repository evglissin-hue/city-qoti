## Unreleased

- Spec v1: principles, game design, architecture, networking, quality, roadmap, the parallel development plan, and cycle briefs for I0 and I1.
- C1 foundation: npm workspaces monorepo, I0 contracts and registries, deterministic sim with worldgen v0, RoomCore with the local relay and a Cloudflare Durable Object, the client (Three.js placeholders, tools, Preact UI) with online co-op and offline solo, gallery, bench, end to end tests and CI.

- C3D-1 world look: smooth storybook terrain (soft slopes, rocky cliffs, curved river banks, painted ground with mottling, wildflowers, field furrows and crops), roads painted with ruts and rounded joins plus plank bridges, water with depth, foam, ripples and sky reflection, varied forests with low detail in the distance, scattered nature props, the spec's dawn to night lighting with a sky dome, ground glow pools at night and a seasonal grade hook.
