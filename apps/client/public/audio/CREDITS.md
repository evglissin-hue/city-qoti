# Sound credits

Generated by `npm run audio:fetch` from `tools/audio/manifest.json`; do not edit by hand.

The ambience and effects are synthesized in the browser as the game plays (`apps/client/src/audio/synth`), so they have no recordings to credit. The music's piano, double bass, strings, flute, clarinet, harp and marimba are samples from the VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (CC0), credited in `samples/CREDITS.md`; its other instruments are synthesized.
